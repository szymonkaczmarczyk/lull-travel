Lull/About

Three people and one rule.

Nothing goes on the list until someone here has slept in it — usually in the worst month of the year rather than the best. It is a slow way to build a catalogue. It is the only part we refuse to shorten.

01

Quiet is the product

The first question is never about the building. It is what happens when you step outside at seven in the morning. If the answer is a car park or somebody else's balcony, the conversation ends there.

02

Run by someone who lives there

We have very little interest in inventory managed from an office in another country. The places that work are the ones where a person with a name notices you arrived late.

03

The downside goes first

Every listing has a line called the catch, and we write it before we write anything else. Guests who know in advance almost never mind. Guests who find out on arrival always do.

Since 2017

It started because a cabin was impossible to book.

A fisherman's cabin in Lofoten, no website, a phone number that rang in a shop. It took nine days and two wrong numbers to reserve four nights, and it was the best week either of us had had.

Nine years later the list is fourteen places across eight regions, and the job is still mostly the same job: knowing who to call, and being honest about what you are walking into.
