The catch

We tell you what is wrong with the house before you book it.

The cottage in Kerry rains for six days out of seven. The camp in the Kalahari has no fence and you do not walk between tents alone after dark. The loft in Nusfjord smells of drying cod from February to May and it is not subtle.

Guests who know the downside in advance almost never mind it. Guests who find out on arrival always do. So it goes at the top of every page, in its own box, before the photographs.

How it works

Four steps, one person, no booking engine.

You will speak to the same person from the first email to the day you fly home. There is no portal, no app, and no confirmation number to memorise.

01

Tell us roughly

A month, a rough number of people, and what you want the week to feel like. That is genuinely enough to start — we will fill in the rest.

02

We send three

Never a list of twenty. Three places, each with a reason it is on the shortlist and a plain sentence about what is wrong with it.

03

We handle the difficult part

Ferry timings, the 4×4 with proper winter tyres, the caretaker who meets a late plane, the dinner that does not move from 18:30.

04

You go quiet

One number to call if something goes wrong. Otherwise we leave you entirely alone, which is what you paid for.
