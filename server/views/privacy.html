Privacy policy.

What we collect when you send an enquiry, why, where it goes and how long we keep it.

Last updated [[publication date]]

1. Who is responsible

The controller of your personal data is [[legal company name]], [[registered address]], tax number [[NIPC]]. For anything about your data, write to hello@lull.travel.

2. What we collect

  • from the Plan a trip form: your name and email, and optionally a region, a month, a stay, the size of your party and a message,
  • the emails we exchange with you about that enquiry,
  • technical data your browser sends with every request, such as your IP address, kept briefly to limit abuse of the form.

Only name and email are required. Please do not put health details or other sensitive information in the message. If someone in your party has access needs, a short note such as “avoid stairs” is enough.

3. Why and on what basis

  • to answer your enquiry and put together a shortlist, because you asked us to (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR),
  • to protect the site and the form from spam and abuse, our legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR),
  • to keep records we are legally required to keep if your enquiry becomes a booking (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR).

We do not send newsletters, follow-up sequences or marketing, and we do not sell data.

4. Who else sees it

  • our hosting provider [[hosting provider]], which runs the server,
  • Supabase, which stores enquiries and serves the hero video on the home page,
  • Resend, which delivers our emails,
  • the owners of a stay, only when you ask us to check dates with them.

Each provider processes data on our instructions under a data processing agreement. Where data leaves the European Economic Area, the transfer relies on the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses.

5. How long we keep it

Enquiries that do not turn into a trip are deleted [[24]] months after our last message. Booking records are kept for as long as tax law requires. Abuse-limiting data is kept for one hour.

6. Cookies

The public site sets no cookies and uses no analytics or advertising trackers. The staff admin panel uses one strictly necessary session cookie. If that ever changes, we will ask for your consent first and update this page.

7. Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, delete or export your data, to restrict how we use it, or object to processing based on our legitimate interest. Write to us and we will reply within one month. You can also complain to the Portuguese data protection authority, the Comissão Nacional de Proteção de Dados (cnpd.pt), or to the authority where you live.
